# 60-Second Demo Script

A word-for-word script for your launch video. Designed to be read in **60 seconds** at a normal speaking pace.

## Setup before recording

1. Open `http://your-railway-url.up.railway.app/`
2. Have screen recorder ready (Loom, QuickTime, or OBS)
3. Make sure browser zoom is at 100%
4. Close all other tabs

## Script (with on-screen action notes)

> **[0:00–0:08]** _On screen: landing page hero_
>
> "This is AI Workflow Studio. It's a tool I built for chaining AI prompts into repeatable pipelines."

> **[0:08–0:18]** _Click "Try the Workflow Builder". The builder loads with one empty step._
>
> "The idea is simple. Instead of one giant prompt, you break the work into steps. Each step does one thing, and feeds its output into the next."

> **[0:18–0:30]** _Click the "Blog Post Pipeline" template chip. Steps populate: Research → Outline → Draft → Polish._
>
> "Here's a pre-built template — Blog Post Pipeline. Research, outline, draft, polish. Four steps, each with a focused instruction."

> **[0:30–0:42]** _Highlight the Initial Input field. Click Run._
>
> "I give it an input — let's say, 'the future of solo developers' — and hit run. Each step executes in sequence. You see the output of every stage."

> **[0:42–0:52]** _Show the results panel filling in. Click into one of the result cards to show the intermediate output._
>
> "I can inspect what each step actually produced. If the outline's weak, I tweak the outline prompt. If the polish step over-corrects, I rewrite that instruction. Total transparency."

> **[0:52–1:00]** _Cut back to landing page or just say to camera._
>
> "I'm Danny Aguiar — I design these AI workflows for teams. If that sounds like something you need, the link's in the description."

## Tips

- **Don't read it.** Memorize the beats, then talk naturally.
- **No "um"s or "uh"s.** Record in chunks if needed and stitch them.
- **Hide your bookmarks bar** — looks more professional.
- **Use a clear, neutral cursor** — no animated cursor extensions.
- **Aim for 55–65 seconds.** Under 60 is best for LinkedIn auto-play.
